1. What is the tightest bound Big-O runtime of random_int_list()?
O(n). The function loops exactly length times and performs a constant amount of work during each iteration (a call to randint and a call to append).

2. What is the tightest bound Big-O runtime of time_trial()?
O(n log n + t log n + tn)
n = data_size
t = num_trials
